# Worklog

This section records the weekly progress of the **Cloud-Native AI Fitness Assistant on AWS** internship project, from Week 1 to Week 12.

{{% notice note %}}
Dates for each week are marked as `[TODO_DATE]` until the actual internship schedule is confirmed. Do not replace them with invented dates.
{{% /notice %}}

Each weekly log includes the objectives, a task table with dates and references, and the results (theory, hands-on work and how it applies to the project). The module weeks also record the difficulties met, how they were solved and the AWS services learned, and most weeks end with links to the related [Workshop](../5-Workshop/), blog or event pages.
