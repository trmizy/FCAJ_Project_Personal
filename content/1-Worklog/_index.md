# Worklog

This section records the weekly progress of the **Cloud-Native AI Fitness Assistant on AWS** internship project, from Week 1 to Week 12.

{{% notice note %}}
Dates for each week are marked as `[TODO_DATE]` until the actual internship schedule is confirmed. Do not replace them with invented dates.
{{% /notice %}}
