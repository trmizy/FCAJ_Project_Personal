Before following the [Deployment Guide](../5.4-Deployment-Guide/), make sure the following are in place:

1. [5.3.1 AWS account and permissions](5.3.1-AWS-Account-Permissions/) — the account, the IAM identity used in the console, and the one-time model access steps for Amazon Bedrock.
2. [5.3.2 Deployment region](5.3.2-Deployment-Region/) — why everything runs in `ap-southeast-1` (Singapore), including Bedrock and OpenSearch Serverless.
3. [5.3.3 Local tools](5.3.3-Local-Tools/) — what is needed on the workstation to build the Lambda artifacts and container images.
